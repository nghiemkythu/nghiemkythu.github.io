Nghiem Tuong Diep.

Ph.D. Student · Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence

I am currently a first-year Ph.D. student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). My research focuses on embodied intelligence, action-oriented world models, and multimodal learning.

I aim to build embodied agents that preserve spatial understanding, learn physical interactions across embodiments, and make reliable decisions under uncertainty.

My research interests include:

  • Embodied policy learning — preserving spatial understanding in vision-language-action models and developing generalizable policies with uncertainty-aware, metacognitive decision-making.
  • Action-oriented world models — learning geometric latent actions across embodiments and physics-aware dynamics for contact-rich manipulation.
  • Geometry and graph learning — structured representations for robotic interactions and medical AI.
