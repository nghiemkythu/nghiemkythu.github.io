Nghiêm Diệp Tường

I am a Ph.D. student at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI). My research focuses on world models, embodied intelligence, and multimodal learning.

I am interested in how models can understand interactions, predict their consequences, and support reasoning and planning in the physical world.

My research interests include:

  • World models for embodied intelligence — learning dynamics that support long-horizon reasoning and planning.
  • Latent action learning — learning transferable actions across different embodiments.
  • Geometry and graph learning — structured representations for robotic interactions and medical AI.
